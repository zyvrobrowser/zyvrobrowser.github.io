QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The quick version.

Is Zyvro free?+

Yes. Zyvro is designed to be free and open source.

Do I need an account?+

No. Workspaces and browser data work locally without requiring sign-in.

What platforms are supported?+

Windows is the primary local build today. macOS packaging is planned separately.

Is version 2 ready for daily use?+

Not yet. Version 2 is an alpha preview while its interface and features are tested.
